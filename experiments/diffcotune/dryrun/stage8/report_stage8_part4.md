## 6. Safety note, safety preview, resource incident

**Facts from the dry runs (frsim, nothing flown).** No dry-run flight crashed. A1 is a *floor* flight at the default gains in this dry-run plant (37 cm 3-D error: the plant P1 has thrust 0.8 and motor lag 45 ms against the model's calibration), so every round of every run has at least one floor flag from A1; the floor flags beyond A1 are the signal. Paper S0: floor flights in A1 (all rounds) and A1, A8, A4 in round 5; largest gain jump per round 14 %, 46 %, 6 %, 3 %, 4 %. Paper S2: 2 %, 7 %, 34 %, 6 %, 1 %. "Ours" is limited to ±25 % per round and reaches the limit (S0 ours: 25 % in rounds 0–2). A lab session sees up to 3 attempts per flight (the first plus at most 2 repeats, protocol): a crashed, tilted or floor flight is repeated and not used for the fit.

**S0 "ours" round 3 (15.96 cm).** The flight of round 3 uses θ₃, computed at the end of round 2 (fit 727 s, tune 764 s, normal times); the slowness of the *fit of round 3* (3899 s, during the overload) came after that flight and did not change θ₃ or the real cost of round 3 (the computation is deterministic). What happened in round 3: A1 got worse (15.85 → 34.45 cm, a floor flight) while the other four scenarios improved (A8 8.60 → 6.56, CIRCLE 2.92 → 2.58, FIG8 2.99 → 2.62, A4 8.67 → 5.49 cm); the largest gain change to θ₃ was 25 % (the limit; factors to θ₂: 1.05, 1.24, 1.13, 1.25, 1.25, 1.25, 0.80, 0.90, 0.80, 1.03). The rerun of the S0 "ours" dry run (clean machine) reproduces or not rounds 0–3: see §5.
