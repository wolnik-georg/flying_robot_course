`usd_check` reads the gains of the axis that was excited: the y chirp (roll) checks `KR_x / Kw_x`, the x chirp (pitch) checks `KR_y / Kw_y` (tested on frsim files for F6 and the x axis). NO-GO: stop and read the failed check. After each flight: power off, card out, `copy_usd_log.py` byte-verified, then `usd_check`. Nothing flies the next item without GO.

## 3. Aborts (every flight; `chirp_flight.py`, mock-tested end to end, `u3_abort_test2.log`)
Triggers: key `q` (land) / `e` (emergency stop, last resort, the drone falls); telemetry tilt > 15 deg or |rate| > 300 deg/s for > 0.3 s; telemetry silent > 0.5 s; **position excursion > 0.3 m from the hover point** (session safety limit, user-approved 2026-11-28); **z < 0.25 m after the takeoff wait** (protocol floor rule); uSD start not verified in hover. Sequence: hold burst at the CURRENT position (0.3 s) -> `notifySetpointsStop` -> HLC land; last resort `cf.emergency()`; `usd.logging 0` in `finally` on every exit path. The script refuses to start without a terminal.
