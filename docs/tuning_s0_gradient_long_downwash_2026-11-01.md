# S0 gradient tuning, part 2: long horizon and per-scenario downwash (2026-11-01)

Continues [`tuning_s0_gradient_2026-10-31.md`](tuning_s0_gradient_2026-10-31.md). Method source: von Prittwitz 2024 (window schedule, differentiable copy) and DiffTune (projected update). CMA-ES is not part of the shared papers; its results are kept only as a black-box reference. The differentiable copy lives in `experiments/tuning/diff/` and does not touch the controllers.

## 1. What was added
| Item | Files |
|---|---|
| Long horizon: whole flights (takeoff, hold, scenario, landing) chained in 1.5-4 s chunks with a carried state (truncated BPTT) | `diff/train_long.py`, `diff/traj.py` (`sample_episode`), `diff/run_long_s0.sh` |
| Downwash in the differentiable plant: NS2 v2 force (z-only, gated), plume torque, one-ms torque lag, as in `plant_core.py` | `diff/ns2_torch.py`, `diff/s0_plant_torch.py`, `diff/check_dw_equiv.py` |
| Per-scenario training (A1, A4, A8) on banks of recorded top-drone instances | `diff/build_bank.py`, `diff/train_scen.py`, `diff/run_scen_s0.sh`, `diff/rank_diff_scen.py` |

Equivalence: with downwash on A8 the differentiable plant differs from the firmware plant by at most 0.16 mm (noise off). Banks (`out/bank_*.npz`, 116 MB) are regenerated by `build_bank.py` and not committed.

Setup: 3 seeds per scenario, 250 iterations, 160 rows, chunks of 1500 ms, robust loss (0.5 mean + 0.5 worst-25 %, tilt hinge above 20 deg, stress rows), GPU (CUDA graphs plus `torch.compile` per 2 ms block). 15-25 minutes per run.

## 2. Long horizon, solo (no downwash)
Reproduces the robust solo gains of part 1 (`diff_s0L_*`, ranked in `diff_s0L_rank.json`); used as the check that chaining windows is sound. Solo-tuned gains fail with downwash (12 of 18 cells crash), hence the per-scenario runs.

## 4. Reading
- The gradient method (differentiable copy, chained windows, projected updates) gives the best S0 gains on A4, ties CMA-ES on A8, and is clearly worse on A1.
- A1 weakness is probably data and horizon: the A1 bank has only 4 instances and the slow hover oscillation may not fit 1.5 s chunks. Not yet tested.
- Plant results only. Tuned sets still crash at the stress extremes (lag 60 ms, state delay 60 ms). Hardware untouched; plant gate only partly met (9/16).
- Candidates for hardware confirmation by firmware score: gradient s2 (A4), gradient s1 or CMA-ES (A8), CMA-ES (A1).
