\chapter{Two-Robot Experimental Results}
\label{ch:results2}

% CHANGED (P6-02): internal campaign label removed.
% CHANGED (round 3, D-13 = (b)): phase 1.
This chapter reports the first phase of the study, the systematic comparison on the two-vehicle
team. Chapter~\ref{ch:setup}
fixed the platform, scenarios, metrics and protocol; Chapter~\ref{ch:control}
defined Strategies~0--4, of which Strategies~0--3 are compared. The sections below follow the analysis
from aggregate tracking metrics through control effort, the predictive residual path, and the
attribution of effects to a loop, to a summary of when each strategy is preferable. Research
questions RQ1, RQ2 and RQ4 (Section~\ref{sec:intro-rq}) are answered primarily here; RQ3 is
deferred to the multi-robot results chapter.
The null scenarios that set the measurement floor are flown in the second phase. In this chapter the
results are therefore read against a provisional floor, the repeat-to-repeat variation within a
cell (Section~\ref{sec:setup-stats}); the multi-robot results chapter reads them again against the
floor of the null scenarios. Every ``negative answer'' below refers to the provisional floor.

All metrics are those defined in Section~\ref{sec:setup-metrics}, computed per flight and summarised
per (scenario, strategy) cell as specified in Section~\ref{sec:setup-stats}.

\section{Experimental campaign overview}
\label{sec:res2-overview}

% CHANGED (round 2, author decision 3): nicknames and internal labels removed; D-05 content unchanged.
The comparison flights use two brushless vehicles, the study vehicle in the lower position of
vertical stacks and a second vehicle on top, with external pose from a four-camera OptiTrack system and
onboard logging at \SI{500}{\hertz} on micro-SD as the source of record (Section~\ref{sec:setup-platform}).
Radio telemetry is for monitoring only; thesis metrics are computed from merged uSD logs after role
alignment, using the same pipeline documented in Chapter~\ref{ch:setup}.

Scenarios are drawn from the frozen formation library (Chapter~\ref{ch:setup}, Section~\ref{sec:setup-scenarios}):
the training and comparison matrix prioritises vertical separation and downwash-dominated geometries (e.g.\ A1,
A3, A4, A7); the null coplanar controls (C1--C3) that bound the measurement floor are flown in the
second phase. Each

selection so session effects do not align with strategy choice (Table~\ref{tab:setup-threats}).

The compared set follows Chapter~\ref{ch:control}: Strategy~0 (uncompensated geometric baseline),
Strategy~1 (INDI), Strategy~2 (geometric + learned residual) and Strategy~3 (INDI + learned
residual), one controller with one shared gain set frozen after the hardware gate.

\section{Predictive strategy (Strategy~2)}
\label{sec:res2-nn}

% CHANGED (round 2): prediction-off flights are those of Strategies 0 and 1 (Ch5 Sec. setup-campaign).
The learned model is evaluated in two layers. On \emph{prediction-off} flights, those of
Strategies~0 and~1, the prediction is recorded but does not drive the loop; these flights measure
model quality as the prediction error $\epsilon_{\mathrm{pred}}$ of Section~\ref{sec:setup-metrics},
independent of loop gain. On \emph{prediction-on} flights, those of Strategies~2 and~3, the
prediction drives the controller; Strategy~2 is compared with Strategy~0 on matched scenarios. RQ1
and RQ2 both depend on the second; the first explains it.

\section{Loop attribution}
\label{sec:res2-attribution}

% CHANGED (round 2; D-01 resolved): the controller subtracts the residual by construction, so the
%   sign check becomes the expected direction of the Strategy 1 effect.
Two checks connect the measured differences to the mechanisms of Chapters~\ref{ch:model}
and~\ref{ch:control}. First, the direction of the effect: Chapter~\ref{ch:model} predicts that, with
the residual subtracted, the formation-induced displacement in a quasi-static stack is smaller under
Strategy~1 than under Strategy~0 during the transient, while the vertical integral that both share
removes a constant offset in either. Second, the loop that carries an effect: the configurations
with switch A or switch B alone (Section~\ref{sec:ctrl-runtime}) are diagnostic, not strategies,
and are used only to attribute a Strategy~1 effect to the force or the moment term.

\section{Hybrid strategy (Strategy~3)}
\label{sec:res2-hybrid}

Strategy~3 is compared with both of its parents on matched scenarios: with Strategy~1, which has the
same measured terms, and with Strategy~2, which has the same prediction. Chapter~\ref{ch:control}
derives what to look for (Section~\ref{sec:ctrl-s3}): when the thrust has settled, the prediction
acts on the measured-residual loop as an offset of its acceleration reference, which the vertical
integral removes; where the prediction leads the filtered measurement in time, in the transient
scenarios, Strategy~3 can differ from Strategy~1.

\paragraph{Negative answer.} If Strategy~3 does not differ from Strategy~1 beyond the provisional
floor (Section~\ref{sec:setup-stats}), the prediction adds nothing to the measured terms at the
settings flown, and the result is reported as such.
