## 6. FBL rework: back to the controller the authors flew (2026-10-24/25)
- **Cause:** the control law was the authors' code, but our inner loop (P-only rate loop, Eq. 19) was not what they flew; on hardware they sent thrust + body rates to the stock Crazyflie rate PID. Our drone needs a small steady body torque; a P-only loop turns it into yaw drift (up to 66°) and a sideways offset (y 15–80 cm RMS).
- **Now:** FBL = the authors' structure: their control law, the stock rate PID (bit-exact against the firmware), physics model + trained 4-neuron network (their training method, our flights), FBL from takeoff to landing like S0–S3, no extra limits; S0–S3 unchanged.

| Simulation (our drone's torque need, our gains, 44 ms motor lag) | flown 2026-10-24 | authors' structure |
|---|---|---|
| steady sideways offset | 18.1 cm | 0.15 cm |
| max yaw | 26.9° | 0.11° |

![simulation: flown inner loop vs the authors' structure](assets/2026-10-29/fbl_rework_sim_traces.png)

- **Still different from the authors:** gains (theirs unpublished; their code gains are unstable in our simulation, both are flown), C_D (ours 0.55, theirs 0.236, both flown), network weights trained by us, onboard instead of laptop + Vicon, downwash term only with a neighbour above within 0.6 m.
- **Next lab (2026-10-26):** refly the 2026-10-24 cells; validation = the paper's own hardware test (two drones stacked on a line, dz 0.4 / 0.3, without model / physics model / physics model + network, 5 flights each) against the paper's Fig. 3.
