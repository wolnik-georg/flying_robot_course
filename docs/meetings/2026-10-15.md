## Questions:
- **INDI + NS2 (S3): how do the two corrections combine?**
  - **Situation**
    - the vehicle below a crossing feels a downwash force `d`
    - INDI measures it (filtered, 30 Hz) and subtracts it; NS2 predicts it (`â`) and subtracts it
    - the measurement does not shrink when the thrust is raised, so it keeps seeing the full `d`
  - **Option A — plain sum (what is flown now)**
    - command: `a_d − â − d_measured`
    - both subtract roughly the same force, about twice the disturbance during a crossing in principle
    - simplest, no extra code; flown: dip −1.9 cm (n = 2), upward overshoot up to +5.7 cm
  - **Option B — INDI only corrects what NS2 missed**
    - command: `a_d − d_measured,f − (â − â_f)`: the measurement carries the slow part, NS2 supplies only the fast part the 30 Hz filter delays
    - each force is counted once; needs one extra filter and a few lines in the NS2 hook (image rebuild)
    - not flown yet
  - **Plan if no preference:** fly both on A8 and A1 in the same session (S3-A vs S3-B) and compare dip and overshoot
  - **Question:** which of the two is the S3 of the study, or do we report both?
