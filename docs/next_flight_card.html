The bug that cost most of the session — ours

fixed, worth remembering

Every flight crashed at a fixed elapsed time regardless of scenario length. Two theories were investigated and were wrong: battery sag (a symptom — the violent correction draws the current) and an apply() controller-identity race (a genuine bug, fixed and kept, but not the cause). The decisive test was flying A1, a pure hover with no trajectory motion, which failed identically and eliminated trajectory content, crossing geometry, downwash, dz, passes, controller and gains in one flight.

Root cause: usec.reset was broadcast mid-flight, and the high-level commander's entire time base is that same clock (float t = usecTimestamp() / 1e6;) with t_begin stored from it at takeoff. Zeroing it in the air makes piecewise_eval compute t − t_begin ≈ minus several hundred seconds and hand the controller a garbage setpoint — motors cut or the vehicle tumbles within one tick. Fixed by broadcasting it once on the ground before takeoff. Post-fix flights returned to the pre-bug baseline exactly.
