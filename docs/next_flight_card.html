C.1 — what each scenario teaches (one line)

  • A7 — Top and bottom stay aligned while dz sweeps 1.10→0.10 m; one flight covers almost the whole vertical separation axis inside the neighbour gate.
  • A1 — Fixed vertical stack, both hover ~15 s; pure dz at ~zero relative velocity (static downwash curve).
  • A3 @ dz 0.25 — Top hovers, bottom translates back and forth; repeats sweep relative horizontal speed at close range (velocity term for the net).
  • A3 @ dz 0.40 — Same pattern at mid separation and mid speed; fills the middle of the dz–speed grid.
  • A2 — Both drones follow the same circular path; own motion with near-fixed relative state (separates “I’m moving” from “neighbour is moving”).
  • A4 — Stacked but laterally offset (≤0.15 m), lemniscate motion; excites small dx/dy inside the 0.2 m gate (mandatory for lateral falloff).
  • C5 — Solo cf5 near the floor; no neighbour — data for the ground-effect branch phi_G, not downwash.
  • A8 — Crossing at wide span: good for controller demos and docs/24; not for C.1 training (neighbour gated out most of the run).
