3 — Stop rules

  • Any Assert failed, rate is off, or silent /cf5/pose: land / power-cycle, send the log, do not fly.
  • cf5 tumbles again: stop; do not retry the same config. Check the pose bag (did a pose jump onto the other drone?) and vbat first.
  • Abort at |roll/pitch| > 25° for 0.5 s or z < 0.25 m. Stop after 2 crashes.
  • After a crash: toggle stabilizer.estimator 1 → 2 before the next flight (stuck EKF).
  • Never broadcast usec.reset mid-flight.
