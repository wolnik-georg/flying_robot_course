## 2. The geometric-crash root cause, in detail

Initially attributed to `kr_geo`/`kw_geo` being brushless-specific and never validated for
standard. **Checked with git history and that turned out to be wrong**: `kr_geo=0.010`/
`kw_geo=0.00110` is bit-identical to the compile-time constant active on standard back on
2026-07-02 (commit `b8bd603`, "all flights stable") — that exact number did fly clean on
standard before. The real cause: everything *around* that gain in the shared
`all.firmware_params` block (`fc_bw=206`, `clamp_en=11`, `res_fc`/`res_clamp`, `tau_xy_max`,
`thrust_max`, notch settings) has been iterated on and validated only against brushless since
July, and standard inherited the whole current configuration the moment it was enabled — not
just one old, still-valid gain sitting in isolation. This is a genuine finding worth
remembering: shared-`all:` configs drift toward whichever platform is actively being tuned,
silently, and re-enabling a dormant platform inherits ALL of that drift at once, not none of
it.
