**A7-specific fix, same day (`crazyswarm2` `782cea0`):** the 25 Sep geofence revert
(`FLIGHT_SPACE['z']` back to 1.70 m) had an unintended side effect — A7's own
`apply_a7_lab_defaults` derived its formation height from that *general* ceiling
(`max_base = z_hi - dz_start`), so restoring it silently pushed A7's computed height back to
0.5 m (top ≈ 1.60 m) — higher than A7 has ever flown, and inside the ≈1.25–1.30 m zone its own
docstring says loses mocap track. Checked every scenario's logged flight history: **no scenario
has ever needed the *general* ceiling tighter than 1.70 m** — A7 is the one genuine exception,
and it needed its own fixed constant, not a shared one. Fixed: `A7_TOP_MAX = 1.20` (matching the
one proven-safe A7 flight, 23 Sep 19:11, top≈1.28 m), independent of `FLIGHT_SPACE`. Re-verified
live: A7 now computes `height=0.1, top=1.20` regardless of the general ceiling; A1/A3/A4/A8
unaffected. **Policy going forward:** keep the general geofence at its proven-good default; any
scenario that genuinely needs tighter constraints gets its own fixed, scenario-specific override
(same pattern C5's `--z-floor` already uses) — never a blanket change again. Full account:
`docs/07` History (55).
