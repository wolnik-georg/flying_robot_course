# Lab Session — 18 September 2026

**Goal at open:** re-fly the 2-drone full-INDI A8 gate (step 1 of the Next Flight Card),
then work down the checklist. **Outcome:** the gate was reached and passed twice, but the
session was then derailed by a runaway-climb failure that took ~10 flights to root-cause.
Four real bugs found and fixed; `cf_second` swapped to brushless.

---

## 1. What happened, in order

**Config bugs cleared first.** `cf_second`'s `rpm:` log topic lived in the shared `all:`
block but logs DShot-only variables (`motor.m1_rpm..m4_rpm`) that only `cf231_active`'s
firmware exposes — it broke `cf_second`'s connect outright. Scoped to `cf231_active`.

**A8 crashed repeatedly at an identical point** (~t=3.6–3.7 s, during the takeoff
climb→`goTo`, before the trajectory even starts), on full INDI *and* geometric, i.e.
independent of controller and gains.

**Root cause #1 — `run_formation.py` ramp flew on the wrong position gains.** The
2026-09-15 refactor (`89be0f9`) replaced `cf231_active`'s explicit per-robot `pos_gains:`
override with the `resolve()`/`GEOMETRIC_POS_GAINS` substitution, but only wired it into
`apply('scenario', ...)`. `apply('takeoff'/'landing', ...)` were called with no gains at
all, so a drone resolving to geometric flew its whole ramp on the shared INDI-tuned
`pos_gains` (64/5/48/7) — the documented 2026-09-09 root cause #1. Fixed (`9ce06e2`).
**A8 then flew clean twice** (18:31 geometric, 18:39 full INDI).

**`cf_second` swapped to brushless** (it broke as a standard CF2.1). Reflashed from
`firmware_app` — note the build output is `build/cf21bl.bin`, not `cf2.bin` as the
Makefile header claims. Stock-Lee hover on the new frame was clean.

**Root cause #2/#3 — two `simple_flight.py` per-robot-override bugs.** Its three
`_apply_flight_settings()` calls never passed `per_robot=`, so per-robot yaml pins were
silently ignored and the shared `all:` values were broadcast to everyone; and
`_select_pos_gains()` was called with the raw shared `(controller, ctrl_mode)` instead of
the drone's resolved pair, so a drone pinned to geometric still got INDI `pos_gains`.
Fixed (`a9209fe`, `4470738`).

**Height compensation removed.** `cf_second` settled at 1.552 m against a commanded 1.65 m
(which still included the old +0.40 m) — a 0.10 m undershoot, not the original 0.40 m. The
mocap miscalibration the compensation existed for is gone on the new brushless mount;
removed from all three flight scripts (`d824fba`).

**Then the session derailed: runaway climbs.** From ~18:44 onward the drone repeatedly
flew into the ceiling or sideways while its logged position sat at zero. This survived
every variable we could change — full INDI, geometric, **stock Lee**, both scripts, solo
and 2-drone, fresh batteries, a mocap restart. ~10 failed flights.

**Root cause #4 — stuck EKF, never properly reset.** Two defects together:

- `run_formation.py` had **no EKF reset at all** (zero references to `kalman`/`estimator`).
  Every A8/C5 flight ran on whatever estimator state the *previous, crashed* flight left.
- `flight.py`/`simple_flight.py` did reset, but only pulsed `kalman.resetEstimation` —
  already documented in this project as insufficient after a crash: with a bad gyro bias the
  EKF stays frozen and only a complementary-filter re-init (`stabilizer.estimator` 1→2)
  clears it.

The signature is unambiguous: after the first hard crash (18:14) every failing flight logged
a **completely frozen position — 0.57 mm of x/y movement across an entire flight** — while
the vehicle physically climbed into the ceiling. The controller saw a constant position
error and kept commanding climb. Mocap was verified tracking correctly throughout.

Fixed (`8296ad8`): proper complementary→Kalman reset in both paths, plus a **pre-takeoff
gate** in `run_formation.py` that compares each drone's own EKF position against its mocap
pose and refuses to arm on >150 mm disagreement.

**Side investigation — the active-marker deck.** Both drones carry active marker decks
(`activeMarker.front/back/left/right` + `deck.bcActiveMarker` present on all cached drone
TOCs), but the ROS side has never used them: `cf231_active` is `type: cf21` →
`default_single_marker` (one point), `tracking: librigidbodytracker`. An exhaustive scan of
the entire `crazyswarm2` object database — every branch, every commit, every unreachable
object — found only 7 distinct `motion_capture.yaml` versions, **all byte-identical to
upstream**, and only two `robot_types` sets ever (`{cf21, cf21_mocap_deck}`). No
`cf21_active`, ever; same for `flying_robot_course`. The remembered supervisor config
(measured deck, used by `cf232_active`) predates both repos (user history starts
2026-05-17) and must live in an older workspace on the lab PC or in Motive.
Search script committed: `debug/lab_logs/find_active_marker.sh` — the lab PC holds local
branches deleted from origin plus 37 stashes that exist nowhere else.
**Not the cause of this session's failures** — that config has been absent since May, and
flights worked throughout July, September, and several times today.

## 1b. Bonus result — first controlled Strategy 0 vs Strategy 1 comparison

The two clean A8 runs were flown back-to-back, same scenario (`dz=0.25`, `span=1.0`,
`passes=2`), same two vehicles (**both brushless** — the swap was at 17:42, so every flight
from 17:51 on is post-swap), differing *only* in `cf231_active`'s `ctrl_mode`. That makes them
a genuine controlled pair. Measured on the **bottom** drone (the one receiving downwash), over
the crossing window `t = 11–25 s`:

| `cf231_active` (bottom) | roll std | roll peak | pitch std | pitch peak | `a_res_z` peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| geometric (`ctrl_mode=0`), 18:31:15 | 7.13° | 30.0° | 6.15° | 22.9° | −2.81 m/s² |
| **full INDI (`ctrl_mode=3`), 18:39:08** | **2.70°** | **11.0°** | **1.64°** | **6.7°** | **−4.14 m/s²** |

**INDI cuts the downwash-induced attitude disturbance 2.6–3.7×** — and does so while measuring
a *larger* disturbance. `cf_second` (top, stock Lee, no downwash on it) stayed at roll std
1.04° / peak 2.02° in the INDI run, confirming the disturbance is specific to the lower vehicle.

**Why INDI doesn't erase it — structural, not a tuning failure.** INDI is *reactive*: it
estimates the residual from measured acceleration and cancels it, so it cannot act before the
disturbance has been measured (irreducible lag of at least the filter time constant, with
`fc_bw=206` / `res_fc=80` further attenuating and delaying a fast crossing transient). And
**lateral** rejection has to route through the attitude loop to tilt the thrust vector — much
slower than the direct thrust channel that handles the vertical component, which is exactly
the part a crossing loads hardest.

**This is the expected baseline result, not a defect.** The residual ~2.7° is precisely the
headroom Strategy 2 (Neural-Swarm2) and Strategy 4 (NA-INDI) exist to close; if plain INDI
erased downwash there would be no comparison to make. Encouraging for **C.1** too: `a_res_z`
reaches −4.1 m/s² cleanly on the bottom vehicle, so the residual signal to be learned is large
and well-measured.

**Status: indicative, not statistical** — one run per controller. This is a *preview* of C.4,
not C.4; the systematic comparison needs repeats under matched conditions.

Logs: `experiments/logs/A8_cf231_active_2026-09-18_18-31-15.csv` (geometric),
`A8_cf231_active_2026-09-18_18-39-08.csv` (full INDI), their `cf_second` counterparts, and
`A8_2026-09-18_18-31-15.meta.json` / `A8_2026-09-18_18-39-08.meta.json` for each vehicle's
resolved per-drone config.
