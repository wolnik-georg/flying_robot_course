- S3 shows a small upward peak after crossings (up to +5.7 cm): consistent with D-12 (plain-sum hybrid), open with the supervisor.
- NS2 timing on the drone: 526 µs worst case (501 µs mean), about 26 % of the 2 ms slot.
- Sign check of the C implementation: thrust residual follows −rnn.pred with coefficient +0.85 … +1.00.
