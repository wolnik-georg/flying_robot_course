### Tal & Karaman INDI: first attempts did NOT run the controller
- Flights (all on cf5): A8 with Rust (controller 11, 18:14, crashed after about 13 s), a hover pinned with `--pin-controller` (18:18, which pins the shared yaml values 6 / `ctrl_mode` 3, so not a Tal & Karaman test), C hover and C figure-8 (controller 12, 18:25), then a hover, a longer hover and a figure-8 on the debug-config card (18:38–18:41). All of them oscillated at 1.0–1.6 Hz (growing slowly in hover, crash in the figure-8 within 3–6 s).
- **Cause found late in the session (between the 18:41 and 18:53 flights):** cf5 did not have the `_tk` image, so `stabilizer.controller` 11 and 12 were silently ignored and the drone stayed on controller 6 with the INDI-tuned position gains (`kp_xy` 64, `kv_xy` 5), the documented 09-09 ring-up. Evidence: the flight logs carried the geometric signature (`indi.tau` 0, `ctrlTk.*` 0, `alp` and `rnn_pred` live; Omar Rust has the opposite), and `flying_drone_stack/tools/check_active_controller.py` showed no "Using OutOfTree6/7" console line for 11 and 12 (10 and 6 do print theirs). The readback of the parameter is only a cache and proves nothing.
- **Fix:** cf5 reflashed with `…_rpmf_…_ON_tk.bin` (sha256 `cb38f3f4…37f8ca`) from the laptop; the check now prints "Using OutOfTree6 (11) controller" and "Using OutOfTree7 (12) controller". cf_second was not reflashed (it runs geometric only).
- **Consequence:** no conclusion about the Tal & Karaman controller can be drawn from the flights above. The first valid test is the next hover. The earlier conclusions about its stability, the delay analysis and the gain variants (`crazyswarm2/crazyflie/config/study/tk_variants/`) wait for it.
- **Lessons:** read the console line "Using … controller" (`check_active_controller.py`) after every flash and before the first flight of a new controller slot. Never trust the parameter readback. Check the flashed image against the manifest by behaviour, since the hash cannot be read back. Logs showing `indi.tau` and `ctrlTk.*` non-zero are the proof in flight.

### Tal & Karaman INDI: what is known now
1. Attempts 1–3 (A8 Rust, hover, C hover and figure-8, hovers on the debug card) ran controller 6 with INDI position gains because cf5 carried the non-`_tk` image: the firmware ignores `stabilizer.controller` 11 and 12 silently. Their 1–1.5 Hz oscillation was the documented geometric ring-up, not this controller. All stability conclusions drawn from them are void.
2. After the reflash (`check_active_controller.py` prints "Using OutOfTree6 (11)" and "OutOfTree7 (12)"), two C hovers took off on controller 12 and flipped within 1.5 s of motor start (pitch +31° / roll −18° at 0.96 s, upside down at 1.2 s; second flight the same, pitch +54° at 1.07 s). `indi.tau` and `ctrlTk.*` are non-zero, so the controller runs.
3. Before any attitude error, on the ground, the torque command is −0.0023 N·m on x and y (equal), growing within 0.2 s; motors 1 and 3 spin up first while 2 and 4 stay at idle (2.8 k RPM) for 0.25 s. The size equals the torque the RPM asymmetry itself implies, so the command follows the RPM-based torque estimate. My reading (not proven): the law reconstructs the current torque from RPM and adds an increment, and on the ground, where the measured angular acceleration is 0, that estimate feeds itself. The law has no ground handling, no limits and no integral. The last frozen values on the SD card before the crash: thrust command 2.0 N (hover is 0.4 N).
4. Prepared but not flown: takeoff on the geometric ramp and switch to controller 12 at altitude (`simple_flight` default handover; yaml commit `8a888e8`, reverted at the end of the session to the clean geometric yaml `264d5f5`).
5. The gain and filter variants (`crazyswarm2/crazyflie/config/study/tk_variants/`: outer half, attitude half, fc 60) were designed for the oscillation that turned out to be controller 6. They are not motivated by anything measured on the real controller and are parked.
