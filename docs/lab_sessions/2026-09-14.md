## 1. What happened, in order

**Supervisor meeting, same day.** Feedback on the weekly meeting doc (too AI-toned, simplify).

**uSD timing bug, root-caused and fixed (desk time).** Each drone's `usecTimestamp()` free-runs
from its own power-on with no shared reference, so uSD log timestamps carried a constant
per-drone offset. Stock firmware already exposes the fix as a broadcastable param
(`usec_time.c`'s `usec.reset`, documented upstream for exactly this: UAV time sync) — we were
just never calling it. Added `allcfs.setParam("usec.reset", 1)` as its own broadcast right
before the uSD-logging-start broadcast in `formation_flight.py`, mirrored in
`flight.py`/`simple_flight.py` (`crazyswarm2` `932d901`). Not yet flight-tested — worth
confirming today's uSD sync check actually shows a tighter offset than before.
