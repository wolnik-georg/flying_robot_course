## Hardware caveat (user, 2026-10-17)
The bottom drone (cf5) is slightly bent. The top drone (cf_second) is fine. All training data of 2026-10-16/17 therefore has the bent airframe as the bottom (labelled) vehicle, so the labels `a_res = a_imu_f − a_rpm_f` can contain a vehicle-specific offset (thrust/attitude asymmetry) on top of the downwash. Decision: finish the current collection with the drones as they are (no role swap, names and URIs stay bound to the physical drones, the merge assumes cf5 = bottom). After data collection a new stable drone is built and used as the bottom drone for a clean (re)collection; the 2026-10-16/17 bank stays as the first-generation data and is compared against it. Any model trained on this bank (pilot, `ns2_v2`) carries this caveat.
