## 1. What happened, in order

1. **Long connection-hang investigation.** `ros2 launch crazyflie launch.py` hung silently
   after the joystick-driver message, with no further output, no error. Ruled out in order:
   stale ROS processes (three genuinely-stale `ros2 launch ... backend:=sim` processes from
   2026-09-07 were found and killed on a *different* machine during remote debugging — turned
   out unrelated to the actual lab-PC hang), `cf_second` blocking the launch (disabled it,
   still hung), and the yaml/code changes from the last two sessions (none touch radio/CRTP
   code — confirmed by diff review).
2. **Found the drone stuck in bootloader mode**, not running its application at all — this is
   why radio scans found *something* but full CRTP connections (`ros2 launch`,
   `check_usd_deck.py`) hung forever: a basic radio ack can succeed while the higher-level
   protocol handshake, which needs the application's CPU actually running, never completes.
   Root cause of *why* it was in bootloader: an operator diagnostic (`bl.start_bootloader(
   warm_boot=True)`) actively commands a running drone into bootloader mode rather than just
   detecting it — likely self-inflicted during debugging, not a pre-existing fault.
3. **A soft reset back to firmware didn't fully clear it** — connection attempts remained
   inconsistent (alternating between hangs and "Too many packets lost") until a full physical
   power cycle, after which the drone was confirmed out of bootloader.
4. **Root cause of the original hang, once untangled: the wrong firmware platform had been
   flashed** — standard CF2.1 firmware onto the brushless airframe, from an earlier `make
   cload` that didn't target `DRONE=bl` explicitly. Rebuilt explicitly with `DRONE=bl`
   (confirmed by the build's own "Build for the cf21bl platform!" banner) including the
   2026-09-12 notch fix, reflashed with correct power-cycle timing — **connected cleanly**.
5. **Notch test flown at last** — `stabilizer.controller=6`, `ctrl_mode=3` (full INDI, matches
   the config the original crash happened under; an earlier attempt at `ctrl_mode=0` was
   caught and corrected first, since the notch filter only touches the attitude-INDI path and
   wouldn't have been exercised at all under geometric), `notch_en=1`, `notch_f0=6.9`,
   `notch_bw=3.0`. **Crashed — worse attitude oscillation than the original 2026-09-11 crash.
   No log saved at all** (nothing in `Controls/logs/` newer than 2026-09-12), so no
   quantitative account of this crash exists, unlike every previous one this project.
6. **Drone inspected after the crash — no physical damage.** Session closed here; uSD logging
   validation (originally planned for today) deferred to next session.
