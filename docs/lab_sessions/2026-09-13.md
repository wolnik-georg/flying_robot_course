   inconsistent (alternating between hangs and "Too many packets lost") until a full physical

   flashed** — standard CF2.1 firmware onto the brushless airframe, from an earlier `make
   cload` that didn't target `DRONE=bl` explicitly. Rebuilt explicitly with `DRONE=bl`
   (confirmed by the build's own "Build for the cf21bl platform!" banner) including the

## 6. Desk-time follow-up (same day): offline notch analysis — CONCLUSIVE

Why did the notch fix make things *worse* rather than merely "still broken"? Answered, via
`experiments/analysis/notch_filter_bode.py`, which ports the `Butterworth2`/`NotchFilter`
coefficient formulas directly from `firmware_app/src/lib.rs` and computes the real discrete
frequency response at the true 1 kHz tick rate — no flight data needed, this is pure
filter-design math.

**Finding: the attitude loop's own natural frequency (`ωₙ = √kr = 7.80 Hz` at the locked
`kr=2400`) sits almost exactly inside the notch's rejection band** (`f0=6.9`, `bw=3.0`, roughly
5.4–8.4 Hz). Between 6.88 Hz and 6.98 Hz the phase swings from −89° to +85° — a ~175° change
in under 0.1 Hz — and even right at the loop's own 7.80 Hz bandwidth the notch still applies
+58° phase and cuts the signal to half amplitude.

This means the notch was never just fighting an unrelated, external resonance — the "shake"
likely *is* the attitude loop's own closed-loop dynamics (consistent with the 2026-09-11
stage-2e result, where lowering `kr`/`kw` — which moves `ωₙ` — measurably reduced the shake).
Notching a fixed frequency that coincides with the loop's own bandwidth corrupts the loop's
feedback signal with large, rapidly-varying phase distortion exactly at its operating point.
That is a structural problem, not a cold-start/warm-up timing bug — no retuning of
`notch_f0`/`notch_bw` fixes it while `kr`/`kw` keep `ωₙ` at 7.8 Hz, because there's no
frequency gap between the shake and the loop bandwidth to place a notch into.

**Decision: the notch-filter approach is abandoned for this problem.** The `bc7190c` fix
(cold-start warm-up gap) was a real, legitimate bug fix — it just wasn't fixing the actual
cause of instability, which was never the warm-up timing to begin with. The coordinated
`kr`/`kw` + `pos_gains` retuning line from 2026-09-11 is the path forward instead (best result
so far: attempt 4, `483/76` + geometric `pos_gains`, peak oscillation 6.7°, still not a fully
clean pass but real, measured progress in the right direction).
