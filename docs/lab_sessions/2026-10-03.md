### ⚠️ New contradiction (changes the interpretation of step 9 above)
If radio `pos_*` **and** uSD `stateEstimate` are the same firmware variable, then for `cf5` on 10-03 they should agree. They do not:
radio `z` ≈ 0.5 m (hover, then crash dynamics: overshoot 1.06, landing ≈ −0.04) vs uSD `z` ≈ 0.95 m **constant** (±0.02) for 25 s while the drone was on the floor with motors 0 and tilt 100–180°.

### ⚠️ Correction: the position "mix-up" is a CONSEQUENCE of the crash, not its cause
Both radio CSVs share **one clock**, so no alignment is needed. Comparing `cf5` and `cf_second` radio `pos_y/pos_z` directly:

| Flight | liftoff | first tilt >20° | `cf5` still has its OWN position at | `cf5` estimate locks onto `cf_second`'s from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13:10:32 (old full-INDI yaml) | 8.5 s | 12.6 s (right after the 0.5 m crossing) | 13.3 s (y −0.45, z 0.29 vs cf_second y +0.36, z 0.97) | ≈ 15.5 s (+7 s after liftoff), while tilting |
| 13:15:00 (geometric, `ki_z=16`) | 8.4 s | **8.8 s (0.4 s after liftoff)**, z overshoot 0.90→1.06 | 13.2 s (y −0.40, z 0.55) | ≈ 16 s, during the flip (tilt 24° → 177° by 19 s) |

So the sequence is: **`cf5` starts to tumble/oscillate first** (flight 2 at liftoff, flight 1 after the crossing) with a correct own position, and only **afterwards** (≈ 15.5–16 s, as it flips) does its position estimate jump onto `cf_second`'s and stay there until both land. The earlier statement "`cf5` believed it was at the top drone's position → crashed" has the **causality backwards** and is retracted.

**Most likely mechanism (hypothesis, not yet verified):** when `cf5` flips, its markers face away/are occluded; the mocap rigid-body tracker (`motion_capture_tracking`, ICP — the log shows `ICP did not converge for rigidBody cf5/cf_second`; both are `cf21_active`, 4-marker, identical marker layout) re-assigns the lost body `cf5` to the nearest identical marker cloud = `cf_second`. `/poses` then publishes `cf_second`'s pose under the name `cf5`, and the broadcast hands it to `cf5`. This explains why it appears in both flights, only late, in both uSD and radio, independent of firmware/backend.

**What this does to the plan:**
- The **`cpp` ID-path / "mix-up" theory is demoted** (ID consistency was never in doubt; the mix-up is downstream of a tracker loss).
- The **real open cause is why `cf5` tumbled/oscillated in the first place**: flight 2 = liftoff tumble with geometric `ki_z=16` on the flash-RNN build (same signature as the 10-01 geometric A1/A6 tumbles → `ki_z` theory regains weight, **but** the flash-RNN firmware overload is a confounder); flight 1 = full-INDI oscillation right after the crossing on the flash-RNN build (10-02 full INDI with `ki_z=0` was clean on default firmware → flash-RNN overload / crossing proximity suspect).
- Clean separation therefore needs the **default firmware** for the `ki_z` A/B (no network running) — consistent with Bench A first.
- New cheap **bench test (no flight, motors off):** both drones on the floor ~0.5 m apart, `ros2 topic echo /poses`, flip `cf5` by hand → does the `cf5` pose jump onto `cf_second`? If yes the tracker hypothesis is confirmed and a tracker/marker-layout fix (distinct marker patterns, `motion_capture.yaml`) is needed before any more close-crossing flights.

### ⚠️ New real firmware bug found while checking (introduced by the peer-velocity hold I accepted earlier)
In `rnn_predict_eval` the branch `ts_new != pt && dt_ms > 0 && dt_ms < 500` is the **only** place `peer_prev` is updated. After any timestamp discontinuity (a peer silent for **>500 ms**, then returning; or timestamps going backwards), `dt_ms` is ≥500 (or wraps) on **every** later packet, so `peer_prev` is never resynced and the **relative velocity stays frozen at its last value forever** (until `State::reset`). The old code resynced every call. Reproduced on the host harness: peer at +0.5 m/s → 900 ms dropout → stationary peer: `pred_z` stays **−1.4611** (correct, fresh state: −1.5007) and is **unchanged 3 s later**. Worst case a stale `|dvx| > 1.5` gates the neighbour out permanently. Real triggers: mocap occlusion/flip (tracker loses a body), peer packet loss >0.5 s, SIL re-runs. **Fixed 2026-10-03 (Appendix J).**
