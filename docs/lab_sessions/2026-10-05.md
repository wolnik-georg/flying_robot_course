# Lab session 2026-10-05 — NS2 100 Hz test

## 1. Goal
Does the 100 Hz network stop the cf5 crashes? Order: bench A/B/C, A8 rnn.en=0, then rnn.en=1.

## 2. What was flown
- Bench A/B/C done (default + 100 Hz RNN build, hash-checked; stack 8x patch).
- A8 rnn.en=0: 2 clean flights (17:39, 17:41), network runs at 100 Hz, rnn_pred_z tracks measured a_res_z (corr 0.92).
- A8 rnn.en=1: 17:59 clean; 18:01, 18:02 crashed (cf5).

## 3. Findings
- 18:01/18:02/18:18: cf5's own estimate jumps to cf_second's pose in one 2 ms sample before any tilt (uSD), independent of rnn.en. Rosbag 18:34 shows the tracker itself gives cf_second cf5's pose (0.0 cm apart for 37 s). cf5/cf_second share identical cf21_active marker patterns (librigidbodytracker); marker pattern cannot be changed.
- 18:34 and 18:55: cf_second battery collapsed to 2.5-2.6 V (rest 3.75 V at start), radio log ends, tracker loses it and gives it cf5's pose. A1: cf5 flipped 0.42 s later with impact signature (tilt 36->131 deg in 80 ms, gyro 1410 deg/s) = cf_second fell onto cf5. No cf5 pose jump before the flip.
- cf_second uSD logs for 18:34 and 18:55 are missing/0 bytes (consistent with sudden power loss).
- cf5 on A1 (rnn.en=1) oscillated +-40 deg at ~2.4 Hz before the crash; not A/B'd vs rnn.en=0.
- NN sign: prediction matches measured residual (A1: -1.61 vs -1.60 m/s^2, corr 0.76); controller adds a_nn with res_sign=+1 (reinforcing). A8 min z error -11.8 cm (en=1) vs -6.1 cm (en=0). `indi_gains.res_sign=-1` flips only the NN term under ctrl_mode=0. Not yet flown.
