## 1. What happened, in order

**Step 0 done**: lab PC restored to `main`, rebuilt, `cf231_active` reverted off its diagnostic
stock-Lee pin.

**EKF-fix validation, solo `C5` on `cf231_active`**: three attempts. Two showed the exact
frozen/runaway-estimate signature the fix was meant to close (one: x/y std 2mm, never left the
ground; one: x/y std 443mm, real flight to 0.96m — clean). The third produced **zero logged
rows** — not an abort, motors spun and the drone moved (skidded, low thrust) — a second,
mirrored bad-estimate failure, opposite sign.

**Root cause hunt — three hypotheses, tested and mostly falsified in sequence:**

1. *Unregistered markers steal association* — `cf_second` present-but-disabled has no rigid-body
   entry, so its markers sit unclaimed. Registered `cf_second` (both enabled) for the next test.
2. **Falsified.** A1 (2-drone vertical stack hover) with both registered still failed the same
   way — `cf231_active` x/y std reached **14918mm / 4290mm**, z to 49m; `cf_second` never left
   the ground, roll to 180°.
3. *Physical placement precision* (supervisor's own remark: single-drone tolerance ≠ 2-drone
   tolerance) — plausible but not yet tested before the real answer surfaced.

**Parallel track: the stash/object-database search finally paid off.** Two scripts
(`debug/lab_logs/find_active_marker.sh`, `find_active_deck_geometry.sh`) swept every local
branch, the stash reflog, and every object in the repo's database, reachable or not.
**Recovered, from 16 near-identical dangling blobs never reachable from any branch**: a
`type: cf21_active` robot type → `marker: active_deck`, a genuine **4-point** marker pattern
(3.8cm square), predating this repo's own history. Both drones use the current
`default_single_marker` — **1 point** — under the same `tracking: "librigidbodytracker"`
association algorithm. A single point has nothing to disambiguate itself from any other marker
in the volume; this became the leading, and correct, explanation.

**Restored to source** (`crazyswarm2` `3e4d510`): `active_deck`'s real geometry in
`motion_capture.yaml`, `cf21_active`'s robot type in `crazyflies.yaml` — committed but inert
(`cf231_active` left on `type: cf21` pending physical confirmation).

**Physically confirmed both drones carry the 4-marker deck.** Applied to both
(`b4aae3c`, `d8f61b5`). First relaunch: **`rigid body tracker initialization failed`** for
both drones — `librigidbodytracker` needs all 4 configured points to match; one drone had only
**3 markers registering** in Motive. Fixed physically (re-seated/cleaned the marker), confirmed
4 distinct points on both drones, relaunched clean — no more init warnings.

**Then the real collision.** A1 retried post-marker-fix. `cf_second`'s log ends abruptly at
`t=9.044s` — flat on the ground the whole time (z −0.008…−0.022, thrust ≈0) right up to that
instant, no sign of the "crazy maneuvers" reported visually. `cf231_active`'s log shows a clean
climb to z=1.5m by `t=9.04s`, then an **instantaneous jump to z=−0.049** at `t=9.093s`, followed
by sustained ~40–80° roll oscillation for the rest of the flight — a real tumble, not a frozen-
telemetry artifact. The two events are ~50ms apart. Leading explanation: a genuine physical
collision — A1 stacks both vehicles at the same X,Y, only `dz=0.5m` apart, held for 12s; `cf_second`'s
telemetry going dead exactly as `cf231_active`'s flight destabilizes fits impact better than
two independent failures. **Decision: stop 2-drone testing for the rest of the session.**

**Hardware scare.** Back to solo re-validation (stock Lee → geometric → full INDI, one drone at a
time). `cf231_active` stock Lee: near-zero thrust, drone never left the ground, a damped ~8-10°
roll rocking — "3 of 4 motors spinning" observed directly. A physical motor/ESC check came back
clean. Swapped to `cf_second` for the same test: **clean.** Then a **burning smell** was reported
from one drone — flying stopped immediately, physical safety check performed (not detailed
further here; see the operator's own notes). Session continued on `cf_second` only.

**This is the checkpoint.** `crazyswarm2` `4a94a40` is a verified-good config to return to if

**Next**: repeat the identical three-controller sequence on `cf231_active` (subject to the
