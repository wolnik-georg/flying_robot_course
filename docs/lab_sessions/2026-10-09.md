## 4. Pass criteria
- Unified firmware: `read_rnn_timing.py` exit 0, `us_max` ≪ 1000 µs (ideally < 300); `kpos_iz` readable; hover/first flight normal.
- NS2 sanity: dip ≈ −3.4 ± 1.5 cm, no tilt > 25° for 0.5 s.
- Ours INDI ON: no new oscillation, steady z within ±5 cm (10-02 legacy: +4.2 cm), sentinel/random motor-response ratio ≈ 1 (`rpm_sentinel_motor_response.py`).
- Omar exact: steady offset ≈ +20 cm like 10-02 (confirms the unified firmware leaves Omar unchanged).
Abort: |roll| or |pitch| > 25° for 0.5 s, or z < 0.25 m. Stop after 2 crashes; toggle `stabilizer.estimator` 1→2 after a crash. Fresh batteries (rest ≥ 4.1 V).
